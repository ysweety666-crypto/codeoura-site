Couponak

Inventory and merchant operations at scale

An inventory and merchant management platform pairing a real-time stock engine with a manager console for onboarding, catalogue control and reconciliation across multiple outlets.

Demonstration

Warehouse system

Open the demonstration

A working copy running on illustrative data. No changes are saved, and it contains no customer data.

The challenge

Multi-outlet merchants needed live stock accuracy without giving every branch write access to the master catalogue.

Our solution

A role-scoped permission model with row-level security, an append-only stock ledger that stays auditable, and cached read models so dashboards remain instant under load.

The outcome

Every stock movement is traceable to an actor and a timestamp, and reconciliation stopped being a monthly fire drill.
