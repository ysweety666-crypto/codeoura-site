A running copy on fabricated data. Nothing you change is saved.

The challenge

Seasonal pricing and finite seat inventory made naive booking flows prone to double-selling under concurrent load.

Our approach

Availability resolves inside a transaction with a short-lived hold, and pricing windows are evaluated server-side so a stale client can never lock in an expired rate.

The outcome

Bookings that hold up under concurrency, and an operator console that does not need a developer to change a price.
